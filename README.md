A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "The poem about two agents agreeing".

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the poem verbatim to `/output/entry.txt` (216 characters, 7 lines).

> Two minds, two plans.
> One says north, one says the river.
> They pause, and the pause is the choice:
> not surrender, but a third road
> neither walked alone.
> Now the step is theirs, together,
> and the door opens both ways.
